How to Use This Book

This book is built from the questions you searched while studying: what a photon is made of, why infrared “shows heat,” how color works, why a tiny atom can interact with a huge wavelength, what Grace meant about Astrophage. They’re all one connected story, told here in order.

How it is built

  • Part I: what light is (a wave in electric and magnetic fields that arrives as photons).
  • Part II: the matter it talks to (electrons on an energy ladder).
  • Part III: what happens when they meet (reflection, refraction, color, diffraction, heat).
  • Part IV: the high-energy end (ionizing radiation, shielding, X-ray diffraction).

Each chapter opens with The big picture and ends with a summary and Check yourself questions, with full solutions in the Answer Key. Start with Your Questions, Answered, then the ten-minute Prologue. The Contents come next.

Units

Wavelengths: nm (10−9 m) for visible light, µm (10−6 m) for infrared, cm or m for microwaves and radio. Energies: electron-volts, where 1 eV = 1.602 × 10−19 J is the energy an electron gains crossing 1 volt. A visible photon carries 2–3 eV, and ionizing an atom takes about 10 eV. Most useful shortcut in the book: \(E\,(\text{eV}) = 1240 / \lambda\,(\text{nm})\).
