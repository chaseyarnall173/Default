Author's notes:
- Football field: 120 yd × 53⅓ yd including end zones (standard NFL/NCAA dimensions) = 109.7 m × 48.8 m at 0.9144 m/yd.
- Scale-model ratios in Table 4.1 and Figure 4.2 are the author's arithmetic from the radii above (nuclear diameters 2 × 1.2 fm × A^(1/3), proton 2 × 0.84 fm). The proton uses its rms charge radius while heavier nuclei use the uniform-sphere radius, so ratios are good to roughly ±20%.
- 90% radius of hydrogen 1s (2.66 a₀), the 1s–2p dipole matrix element and all plotted densities are computed from the exact hydrogen wavefunctions (figures/scripts/ch04_orbitals.py).
- Some Z = 11–36 ionization energies plotted in Figure 4.6 (e.g. Mg, Al, Si, P, S, Cl, Ca, Sc–Zn, Ga–Kr) are standard NIST ASD values that could not be individually re-checked against the live NIST page in this session.
